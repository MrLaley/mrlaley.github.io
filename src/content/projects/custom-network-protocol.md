## Project Overview

Design and implementation from scratch of a reliable communication protocol in Java, capable of ensuring data integrity in networks with high error rates and instability.

## Technical Highlights

* **Reliable Data Transfer (RDT):** Developed the logic to actively manage packet loss, interference, and disconnections, ensuring robust communication through error-control mechanisms.
* **Socket Programming & Connectivity:** Implementation of the network architecture for node discovery and connection, managing real-time information exchange.
* **Scalability & Concurrency:** Design oriented toward scalability, enabling smooth interaction between multiple simultaneous nodes through the efficient use of multithreading.
* **Resilience Testing:** Simulation of adverse network environments to validate the system's recovery capacity against critical infrastructure failures.
* **Collaborative Development:** Use of GitLab for software lifecycle management, maintaining rigorous version control and effective team coordination.

**Skills:** TCP/IP, Network Protocols, and more.
