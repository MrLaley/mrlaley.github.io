## Project Overview

Collaborative team development of high-performance software to solve the Graph Coloring Problem, a classic challenge in combinatorial optimization and computational complexity (NP-complete).

## Technical & Team Highlights

* **Advanced Algorithms:** Implementation and benchmarking of exact algorithms (Backtracking with pruning techniques) and heuristics to determine the chromatic number of complex graphs.
* **Performance Optimization:** Design of efficient data structures to minimize time and space complexity when processing large-scale graphs.
* **Mathematical Foundations:** Application of discrete structures and graph theory to model constraints and optimize resource allocation.
* **Complexity Analysis:** Rigorous evaluation of algorithmic efficiency across various graph classes and edge densities.
* **Software Verification:** Implementation of cross-validation and rigorous testing within the team to ensure the mathematical correctness of developed algorithms.
* **Soft Skills & Collaboration:** Distribution of complex technical tasks, fluid technical communication for component integration, and joint resolution of algorithmic bottlenecks.

**Skills:** Graph Theory, Algorithm Design, and more.
